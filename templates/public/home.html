Employee gesture tracking

Walking, standing, sitting, bending, running or inactive for long periods. Patterns, not judgements — and an immediate flag if somebody appears to have fallen.

Geofencing

Draw a boundary around machinery, a stock room, a server rack or a staff-only area. Know the moment somebody crosses it who should not.

Crowd management

See where people gather, how many, and whether the crowd is flowing or jammed — the difference between a busy corridor and a crush risk.

Theft detection

Highlights unusual object handling and stock changes for review. It flags risk for a human to check; it never accuses anybody.

Dangerous & unattended objects

Sharp tools where they should not be, a bag left in a corridor, a spill nobody has cleared. Catches things left behind even when no class was ever trained for them.

Sees fire the moment it is in frame, anywhere in the room — including high ceilings and outdoor areas where a smoke sensor is useless.

Our own AI, not somebody else's API

Train Campy AI on your footage

Every model in Campy AI is ours end to end — the architecture, the training loop, the weights format and the runtime. Nothing is sent to a third-party AI service, and your footage never leaves your deployment.

That means you can build a detector for the thing your site cares about: your uniform, your machinery, your stock, your definition of unsafe. Label a few hundred frames in the browser, press train, watch the accuracy climb, and deploy it to every camera with one click.
