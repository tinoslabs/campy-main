How enrolment works
  1. Each photo is scanned for a face using skin-chroma and shape analysis.
  2. The face crop passes through CampyFace, our own embedding network.
  3. The resulting vectors are averaged into one unit-length signature.
  4. Recognition then compares live faces to that signature by cosine similarity.

Because this is an embedding rather than a classifier, adding a person never requires retraining — and the photographs themselves are not kept.
