Choosing classes

Put the "nothing happening" class first. It is used as the negative class when importing dismissed false alarms, and it is usually the biggest class by far.

normal, fire, smoke normal, helmet, no_helmet empty, box, pallet, spill
How much data?

Around 100–300 labelled frames per class gets a usable model for a fixed camera. Frames from different times of day matter more than raw volume — that is what makes it survive dusk.
